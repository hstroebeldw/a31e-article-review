## 2.4 Geology and hydrogeology (add as the last paragraph, after the GRAII values)

The dolomite of the Malmani Subgroup, which underlies Zeerust, lies outside A31E. It covers 629.6 km², or 46.4%, of the 1 357 km² catchment at the start of the modelled reach, and none of it lies in A31E, so all of it is above Klein Maricopoort Dam (Michaluk & Keyser, 1991; Walraven, 1981).

## Notes for the author (not article text)

- **Source of "none in A31E".** The hydrogeological units table of `49e_geology_dolomite_table.xlsx` has 629.6 km² of karstic dolomite in the area of the catchment at F2 and 0 km² in the third area (A31E). Arithmetic: 629.59 / 1 357.26 = 46.4%; F2 minus the dam catchment (1 162.09 km²) leaves 195.17 km² in A31E, and none of the dolomite is there. The Results 6.7 sentence that now carries this ("all of which lies above Klein Maricopoort Dam, while A31E itself contains none") can go, because the Study Area says it.
- **Springs and the Paardenvallei eyes are not in this draft.** My first version added a sentence on them. The name "Paardenvallei eyes" is not in the article text; it came from my own notes of an earlier reading of Pyburn (2015) p. 45, and the statement that they lie in the dolomite above the dam was my inference, not a source. It is removed. Nothing about springs is added to the Study Area unless you ask for it.
- **Reference list.** Because the sentence is gone, this draft cites no new source. Pyburn (2015) is again cited nowhere and leaves the reference list with the others (see `References_and_table_numbers_v1.md`).
- **Rainfall.** Section 2.2 gives 597 mm for the WR2012 MAP (596.7 mm), and the monthly series of the same zone averages 599.0 mm over 1920–2009 (checked in an earlier pass; Figure 6 uses that series). The article text now contains no 599 mm, so nothing needs to change unless a Result quotes the series mean. Say if you want "(the monthly series averages 599 mm over 1920–2009)" after the first sentence of 2.2.
- **Left out on purpose:** springs, the cause of the low inflow in the drought, the Rietpoort Well Field and the 1974 date.
