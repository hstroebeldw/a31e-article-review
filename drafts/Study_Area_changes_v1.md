## 2.4 Geology and hydrogeology (add as the last paragraph, after the GRAII values)

The dolomite of the Malmani Subgroup, which underlies Zeerust, lies outside A31E. It covers 629.6 km², or 46.4%, of the 1 357 km² catchment at the start of the modelled reach, and none of it lies in A31E, so all of it is above Klein Maricopoort Dam (Michaluk & Keyser, 1991; Walraven, 1981). Springs in this dolomite, among them the Paardenvallei eyes, declined to almost nil in 1986 and stayed low until 1996 (Pyburn, 2015:45).
