## 2.3 Topography and drainage (replaces the whole subsection)

A31E falls from the high ground in the south, where the Waterkloofspruit and the Wilgeboomspruit rise, to Kromellenboog Dam in the north-east. A valley crosses the northern part of the catchment from the Doringrivier in the west to the dam (Map 2). Four streams drain A31E. The Klein Marico River flows from the wall of Klein Maricopoort Dam through the valley to Kromellenboog Dam. The Doringrivier drains the western arm and joins it below the dam wall, the Waterkloofspruit joins it further downstream, and the Wilgeboomspruit reaches it at the head of Kromellenboog Dam.

## 2.4 Geology and hydrogeology (replaces the whole subsection)

A31E is underlain by rocks of the Pretoria Group, which are cut by diabase and partly covered by surface deposits (Map 3; Michaluk & Keyser, 1991; Walraven, 1981). Quartzite and sandstone, mainly of the Timeball Hill Formation, form the high ground in the south. The andesitic lava of the Hekpoort Formation lies in the east, mudrock in the centre-east and hornfels south-west of the centre, and diabase occurs in small bodies. Surface deposits, including alluvium, lie mainly along the valley in the north. Groundwater occurrence depends primarily on geology, weathering and effective rainfall (MacDonald et al., 2012:2). On the hydrogeological map, A31E is classified as an intergranular and fractured aquifer and a fractured aquifer, and karst aquifers do not occur in A31E. The mapped median yield of boreholes is 0.5–2.0 L/s over most of A31E and 0.1–0.5 L/s in the rest (Map 4; Barnard, 1999). The National Groundwater Archive (NGA) holds the borehole records of the catchment (DWS, s.a.c). The Department judged that groundwater in the Marico can meet domestic supplies "but not bulk water requirements for purposes such as irrigated agriculture" (DWAF, 2004:55–56).

## 2.5 Soils and land cover (replaces the whole subsection)

The soils of A31E are assigned to the hydrological soil groups B, B/C, C and C/D, which rank soils according to their runoff potential when thoroughly wet, from moderately low in group B to high in group D (Schulze & Schütte, 2023; USDA-NRCS, 2009:7-2). Group C/D lies in the north-east and along the northern edge, group B/C in a belt from the west across the centre, group C in the south, and group B in one small patch in the north-east (Map 5). Natural woodland covers most of A31E, with natural grassland in the south and west. Fallow land, old fields and cultivated land lie mainly along the valley, with small patches of irrigated land, and plantation forest lies in the south. Kromellenboog Dam lies in the north-east (Map 6; DFFE, 2024).

## 2.6 Water use and water quality (replaces the whole subsection)

Of the 3.38 Mm³/a registered in A31E, 69.3% is registered as "scheme" use, 22.2% as river abstraction and 8.4% as borehole use, and 98.9% of the total is for irrigation (DWS, s.a.f). The Department has described return flows from Zeerust and from irrigation that are "being discharged into the Klein Marico River" (DWAF, 2004:86), and it noted that their nutrient and salt content limits their re-use (DWAF, 2004:57). Water quality was monitored at seven sites in A31E between 1966 and 2023 (DWS, s.a.e).

## Notes for the author (not article text)

**What was wrong in the old Section 2, against Sascha's comment**
1. 2.4: the GRAII values are modelled regional data used as model inputs, so they are Methods, and Methods already pointed to them in 2.4. They now sit in 5.1.2.
2. 2.4: the Map 4 caption said "The yield classes are mapped medians, not measured yields", which is a judgement of the data. The caption now says "mapped median" in the title of the class; the yield classes themselves were not described in the text, and are now.
3. 2.5: "classified from satellite images and is not surveyed on the ground" is a judgement of a data set. The first half is a Methods fact (moved); the second half is dropped.
4. 2.5: the text said land cover is natural woodland and grassland, cultivated land and "a small area mapped as irrigated land in the strip below the dam". Map 6 also shows plantation forest in the south, fallow land and old fields along the valley, settlements, and the dam; the text was too thin to describe the map.
5. 2.6: "The national register … records entitlements and not the actual abstraction" is a data limit (moved to Methods). "These return flows are inferred to enter the river upstream of it" is an interpretation; its only other use was in the old Discussion, which is replaced (not in Discussion v8). The sentences pointing to Sections 5.1.4 and 6.8 are dropped. The claim "the irrigation of A31D lies above the dam" has no source in the folder.
6. 2.3: "The Klein Marico River runs the length of the valley" does not match Map 2, where the Doringrivier occupies the western arm and the Klein Marico runs from the dam wall to Kromellenboog Dam. The "modelled naturalised runoff of A31E is 7.21 Mm³/a" is a WR2012 model output that no other section uses, so it is dropped. Say if you want it back.
7. Map 3: the caption said the rock types are combined into four groups, but the legend has eleven classes, and alluvium and surface deposits are separate classes.
