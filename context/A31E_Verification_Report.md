## Numbers to fix or explain

- **Midgley and Pitman peak (889 m³/s).** The article gives none of its inputs (veld-type constant K_T, slope, hydraulic length, distance to centroid), so it cannot be reproduced from Equation 3.34.
- **Inflow series.** The article never says Tables 7 and 10 use series 1 (inflow as recorded, sw1). Say so once.
- **FirmYield sheet versus Sens sheet.** At 0.5 Mm³/a with the rule, FirmYield shows 77 failure months (reliability 0.913) and Sens shows 0.821. The article uses Sens.
- **Wording.** In case D the mean shortfall rises 46% to 63%, so "a half to two-thirds" should read "almost a half". The 19% phosphate exceedance is at A3H042 only (A3H030 is 4%), so "below the upper dam" is ambiguous. "No large urban source" is an inference from the population of 936.
- **From the earlier number check (not re-run).** Table 6 prints inflow 3.33 against 3.32 and storage change +0.36 against +0.35, with no losses row. The 0.84 Mm³/a for 1985-94 is series 1, but the sentence is about the patched flow (1.01). The canal 0.25 Mm³/a is the mean of the 11 counted years but is stated for October 1983 to September 1995 (0.36). The Wilgeboomspruit "a third of its former mean" is 54 to 61%. Six gauge-table cells differ in the last digit.
