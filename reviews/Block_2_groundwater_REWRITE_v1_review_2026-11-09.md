## B. Interpretations the author must decide (best-supported answer in the draft)
- "The catchment probably lies between S and D": true for the baseflow only. On the lowest ground the measured water table is deeper than in both (−23.1 and −11.0 m). v2 states the two facts and says neither structure agrees with both.
- The share of river water is a minimum in both structures (drains only remove water; Leake & Barlow 2013 say pumping can also draw river water into the aquifer).
- "Depth of the ground drain decides the source, conductivity decides the drawdown": rests on one check run and on how the drains are built; drain conductances and outflow depths were not varied. v2 says "in these runs, and partly through the way the drains are built".
- Steady state: v2 adds one sentence that the first period of pumping (water from the aquifer itself) is not shown.
- Reserve paragraph at the end of 6.4: stay or move to 2.4.
