# Panel report: Block 1 water balance, version 10 (9 November 2026, afternoon)

Reviewers: hydrologist-surface-water and academic-editor (citations not re-checked at the author's request; the Pyburn, DWAF, Moriasi and DWS wording was checked in the v8 round). Consolidated by the main session. Version 11 applies the findings below.

## A. Clear errors (all fixed in version 11)
1. 7.1 "the fall during the drought is too large to be an error in evaporation": a new result with no support in Results, and it excludes only evaporation. Replaced by an argument from Table 6.
2. 7.1 "The inflow probably fell because of the dry years of 1982 to 1985 and stayed low because recharge stayed low": contradicted Methods 5.2.4 ("not caused by low rainfall") and ignored 1991 (314 mm, the driest year of 1920–2009). Rewritten from the data. **5.2.4 itself is too strong and needs changing.**
3. 6.3 "With the rule, no firm yield exists, and even at 0.5 Mm³/a ... 0.82": this describes how the rule was built (shares fitted at 2.34), not the dam. Replaced by a limitation sentence.
4. 6.3 "It is about a quarter of the 1.4": "it" referred to 0.44 (31%), not 0.36 (26%); 0.44 holds for series 1 only. Fixed; the comparison moved back to 7.1.
5. 7.1 "A rule fitted on one period does not reproduce the canal volume of the other period well": read as the opposite of Table 8 (three of the five columns pass). Based now on the two fitted rules.
6. 6.2 "Of the 38 years of the raised dam": the dam had 44 years of which 38 have a complete record. Fixed.
7. 6.2 "ten years ... thirteen years": two counts in one paragraph. Thirteen stated once.
8. Table 8 caption: the storage fit is not independent (inflow, pan factors, release table and area curve from the same records). Added.
9. Table 7 rounding sentence and the flagged months added; Table 6 caption says series 1 gives 3.39.
10. 6.2 "99% to 22%": 22% is not the low point (2% in November 1992). Added.
11. Two of the five shortfall years with the dam above 75% lie in the doubtful years 1975–1980. Added.
